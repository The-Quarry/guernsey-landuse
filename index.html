Where the data comes from

The Quarry searched the States of Guernsey planning register month by month for every application validated from January 2015 to October 2026, 17,913 in all, and sorted them by what each one proposes. Each map explains exactly how its applications were chosen and placed.

  • States of Guernsey planning register (Planning Applications Websearch)
  • States of Guernsey Digimap: planning site boundaries and land parcels
  • States of Guernsey Housing Site Availability Framework 2026
  • Guernsey Press and other published reporting, cited on each map
