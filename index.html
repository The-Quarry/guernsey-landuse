The Quarry · Guernsey land use

How Guernsey's land is changing

A weekly series of maps built from every application on the States planning register since 2015, tracing what happens to the island's fields, hedges and banks.

Map 1

Fields into gardens

830 applications to turn fields and farmland into domestic garden, before and after the 2016 Island Development Plan.
Map 2

Hedges, banks and driveways

2,132 applications to create or widen a vehicle access, or take out a hedge or earth bank.
Map 3

New homes from old buildings

223 applications to turn packing sheds, barns and outbuildings into homes, or to replace them with houses.
Data from the States of Guernsey planning register and Digimap. The Quarry.
